How it works

Small, measurable habits beat one-off motivation. Selfward keeps the loop simple.

Take your Day 0 photo

A framing guide and lighting check make your next photos comparable. The photo is saved only on your device.

Pick a few habits

Sleep, strength, skin care, posture, focus, steps and more. A short daily checklist builds your streak — miss a day and your weekly grace day covers it.

See your own change

Slide between Day 0 and today, and watch your weekly trends. The only comparison is with yourself.

What Selfward will never do

These are product rules, not marketing.

No scores, no rankings

It never rates your face or body, never ranks you against others, and has no leaderboards.

Photos never leave your phone

No uploads, no face analysis, no facial templates. Your logs stay on your device too.

No shortcuts that hurt

No calorie restriction targets, no drugs, no surgery advice. Nutrition goals are things to reach, never limits.

No ads, no tracking

One simple subscription pays for the app. We don't sell or share your data.
