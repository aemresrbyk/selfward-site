# Selfward — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2 November 2026._

Selfward is made by Ali Emre Sarıbıyık ("we"). Contact: **aemresrbyk@gmail.com**.

## The short version

- Your **progress photos never leave your device.** We never upload them, never
  analyse your face, and never build a facial template.
- Your habits, check-ins, weight, mood and notes are stored **only on your
  device**.
- We have **no ads, no analytics SDKs and no tracking.** We do not sell or share
  your data.
- We only process what is needed to sign you in and to manage your
  subscription.

## What we process, and why

| Data | Where it goes | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Email address, or the identifier Apple / Google give us when you sign in with them | Supabase (our authentication provider, EU region) | To create and secure your account |
| An anonymous app user id and your purchase / subscription status | RevenueCat, Apple App Store, Google Play | To provide and restore the subscription you paid for |
| Progress photos, habit logs, check-ins, weight, mood, goals, settings | **Only on your device** | To run the app |
| Notification schedule | **Only on your device** (local notifications, no push server) | Reminders you turned on |

We do not collect your location, contacts, health-app data, advertising id or
device fingerprint.

## Payments

Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your card
details. RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt so the app can check whether
your subscription is active.

## Your choices and rights

- **Delete everything on the device:** Profile → Delete all data. This is
  available even without a subscription.
- **Delete your account:** Profile → Delete Account. This deletes your account
  from our server immediately and wipes the app data on your device. You can
  also email us and we will do it within 30 days.
- **Cancel your subscription:** in your App Store / Google Play subscription
  settings. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
- Under the GDPR and Turkey's KVKK you can ask us for access to, correction
  of, or deletion of the personal data we hold about you. Email
  **aemresrbyk@gmail.com**.

## Age

Selfward is for people aged **16 and over**. We do not knowingly process data
from anyone younger.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above and show a notice in the app
for material changes.
